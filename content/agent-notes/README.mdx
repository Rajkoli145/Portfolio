## Organic Growth Principle

Research folders are created **organically** on demand when active research on a specific chapter begins. We do not pre-scaffold empty directories for future chapters.

Each active chapter directory under `research-notes/` maintains four standardized note files:

```text
research-notes/
├── 00-vision/
│   ├── notes.md         # Raw thoughts, literature notes, and emerging concepts
│   ├── hypotheses.md    # Testable conjectures and theoretical models
│   ├── questions.md     # Open research questions and analytical edge cases
│   └── reading.md       # Focused reading list and paper annotations for this chapter
├── 01-what-is-an-organization/
│   ├── notes.md
│   ├── hypotheses.md
│   ├── questions.md
│   └── reading.md
└── (New chapter folders created organically as work begins)
```
