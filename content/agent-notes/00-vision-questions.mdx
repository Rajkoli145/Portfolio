### Flagged in-chapter

**Q0.1** (flagged at Section 0.1, "The Limits of a Purely Technical Framing")
Is individual-agent capability only weakly related to organizational competence — i.e., can a collection of highly capable agents still be organizationally incompetent? Stated as a testable proposition; not yet tested. See H0.1 / E0.1 in `hypotheses.md` for a proposed test.

**Q0.2** (flagged at Section 0.5, "Approach and Method")
Is convergence across independent disciplines genuine evidence of a substrate-independent necessity, or an artifact of a shared intellectual tradition? This is a methodological question about the handbook's own approach, not a claim about organizations themselves — it needs to be settled (or at least addressed head-on) before later chapters lean too heavily on cross-disciplinary agreement as evidence. See H0.4 / E0.4 for one proposed way to test the underlying assumption empirically.
